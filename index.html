Privacy Policy and Terms

Effective date: 10 November 2026

Qervux is designed around device-first privacy: offline, end-to-end encrypted messaging without a central message server.

01

What is Qervux?

Qervux is an offline, end-to-end encrypted messaging app. Messages travel directly between phones or through other Qervux phones.

02

What data is stored?

Your name, @username, an encrypted derivative of your password, photo, “About” text and an optional phone number (used only to find contacts; it is not verified). Everything is stored encrypted on this phone only.

Messages and media are stored only on the participants’ devices and are never sent to a server.

03

Contacts and profile visibility

Contacts access is optional. The list is matched only on your device. People you saved who also installed the app appear. Unknown numbers cannot message you. A short hash of your number may be broadcast to nearby Qervux phones; you can turn this off in Settings.

Your name and number are visible to your contacts. For your photo, “About”, @username and last seen, you can choose Everyone, My contacts or Nobody in Settings > Privacy.

04

Encryption and relay phones

Messages are end-to-end encrypted. Each chat has a 60-digit security code. If it matches the code on your friend’s phone, the chat is protected.

A phone that forwards your message cannot read its content. It can only see the packet size and time. You can turn off relay mode in Settings.

05

Permissions

Bluetooth and nearby devices are used for the mesh connection. Contacts are used to find your friends. Notifications are used for message alerts.

On Android 11 and older, the system requires Location permission for Bluetooth scanning; Qervux does not collect your location. Microphone permission is requested only when you record a voice message.

06

Safety rules

If someone you do not know pretends to be a friend, relative or “support” and asks for money, codes or personal data, do not reply; call your friend to check. Never share your password: Qervux will never ask for it.

Do not open unknown APKs, files or links. Share your live location only with people you know. Block suspicious users.

07

Your rights

You can change your profile, block a contact, clear a chat and delete your account at any time. When the account is deleted, all data on the device is erased.

08

Age limit and parental controls

The app is for users over 16 years old. Parental controls are available for families.

09

Usage rules

Spam, threats, fraud, harassment and illegal content are prohibited. Violations may lead to account restrictions.

10

Changes

You will be notified in the app when this policy changes.
